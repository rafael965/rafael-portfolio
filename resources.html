The Operations Runbook Kit

Four templates for turning work that lives in your head into work someone else can run. Every one came out of a real job — a client intake chain, an executive's calendar, a project coordinator's handover.

They are deliberately tool-agnostic. The value was never the software; it was deciding who owns each step and what “done” means. Nine pages, no signup wall on the content itself — if you would rather just read it, the download link is in the email and there is nothing else in there.

  1. 01Intake to onboarding Seventeen steps from first enquiry to onboarded client, with owner, tool and a “done when” condition per step.
  2. 02SOP template The seven fields that make a written process survive contact with reality — including the one everybody leaves out.
  3. 03Weekly calendar structure Five block types, availability bands, and deadlines held as fixtures. How to protect the work only one person can do.
  4. 04AI prompt workflows Six prompts on a shared Role / Task / Context / Constraints / Output structure: inbox triage, thread-to-actions, meeting brief, report narrative, reply draft, prospect research.

One email with the download link. No list, no sequence, no sharing your address with anyone. Reply “remove” and I delete it.
